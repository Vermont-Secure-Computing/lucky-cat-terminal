LCTerm (Lucky Cat Terminal) is a free and open-source Android Point-of-Sale (POS) application designed for merchants to accept and verify cryptocurrency payments.

It works on Android phones and tablets and is also designed for Android point-of-sale devices equipped with thermal receipt printers.

LCTerm supports multiple cryptocurrencies and payment networks, QR code generation, conversion between cryptocurrencies and local currencies using current market rates, transaction verification, and receipt printing.

Supported cryptocurrencies and payment networks include:

* Bitcoin (BTC)
* Lightning Network
* Litecoin (LTC)
* Dogecoin (DOGE)
* Bitcoin Cash (BCH)
* Dash (DASH)
* Ethereum (ETH)
* Tether (USDT - TRON Network)
* Solana (SOL)
* USD Coin (USDC - Solana)
* Monero (XMR)
* Nano (XNO)

LCTerm is not a cryptocurrency wallet and does not hold customer or merchant funds. Instead, it verifies that customer payments are made to addresses or wallets specified and controlled by the merchant.

To use LCTerm, a merchant requires an API key from Vericeive.com.

Some functionality relies on the DogPay API, a non-free network service.

LCTerm is developed by Vermont Secure Computing and released as open-source software under the Apache License 2.0.
